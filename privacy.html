Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

West Austin Lightworks is a residential Christmas light installation business serving West Austin, Texas. This page explains what we collect when you contact us and how we use it. We keep it short because we keep it simple.

What we collect

When you request a quote, you may give us your name, phone number, email address, property address, and any details you type about your home. We also use privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics to understand which pages people visit — this does not identify you personally.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request: to text or call you, prepare a quote, schedule a walkthrough, and follow up about your service. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.

Text messages

If you check the consent box on our form, you agree that we may text you about your quote and service. Message and data rates may apply. You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP, or ask us to remove your information by emailing the address below.

Who processes your data

Our website and quote form are hosted on Netlify, which processes form submissions on our behalf. Our analytics are provided by Plausible, which does not use cookies or collect personal data. We share your information with these providers only as needed to run the site and reach you.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have, correct it, or have it deleted at any time. Just reach out and we'll take care of it.
